## Flags
- **McKinsey ~$25T total:** I added two snippet figures together; McKinsey does not state that total. Get the exact figure from the PDF before using it.
- **Cut Through currency:** whether the A$5.4B figure is AUD or USD is unconfirmed.
- **Royal Assent date (8 Apr 2026):** not seen on an official page; the 1 Apr 2026 passage date is confirmed.
- **Bain 50% / 16%:** from 2023, not updated.
- **Custody market size:** vendor numbers are inconsistent, so leave them out.
- **EY "% citing custody as a barrier":** not verified.
- **CFA Institute Trust Study:** only the 2022 edition found (83% of retail investors say full fee disclosure is key). Not used.
- **ASIC CSF data:** ASIC has published no official CSF survey since FY19; the totals are Birchal industry data.
